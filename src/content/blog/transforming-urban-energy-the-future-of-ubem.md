Urban Building Energy Modeling, or UBEM, sits at the intersection of building science, urban data, and energy planning. It is a way to represent groups of buildings so that cities, campuses, and districts can explore how demand might change under retrofit, electrification, or growth scenarios.

The future of UBEM is not only higher-resolution geometry. It is better decision use: clearer uncertainty, stronger links to operations and policy timelines, and digital twin workflows that keep models from becoming static reports. Models remain simplifications. They are most valuable when stakeholders can see what was assumed.

TwinBuilt Studio uses UBEM as part of a consulting practice that also includes retrofit analysis and decarbonization strategy. The goal is not a more elaborate dashboard. It is a calmer, more defensible conversation about what to do next.
