Buildings and construction are widely cited among the largest contributors to global carbon emissions. Exact shares vary by study, year, and whether operational energy, materials, or both are included, so TwinBuilt Studio does not treat any single percentage as definitive. What is clear is that building-sector decisions are central to municipal and campus climate plans.

Decarbonization strategies are most useful when they are sequenced. Envelope improvements, systems upgrades, electrification, operations, and district energy options interact. A measure that looks attractive in isolation can shift peaks, alter plant sizing, or delay a better pathway.

A practical strategy starts with the questions the owner can actually decide: which buildings, which years, which constraints, and which evidence is good enough to move. Retrofit pathway analysis and urban building energy modeling are tools for making those trade-offs visible.
