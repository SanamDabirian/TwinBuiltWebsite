The urgency of addressing climate change has never been more pressing for cities and building owners. One promising direction is the careful integration of artificial intelligence into building energy analytics, not as a substitute for engineering, but as a way to organize complex operational and stock-level data.

AI methods can help detect unusual consumption patterns, cluster similar buildings, and support scenario exploration at a speed that manual review cannot match. Those capabilities are only as sound as the meters, models, and assumptions behind them. TwinBuilt Studio treats analytics as decision support: useful when it clarifies options, incomplete when it is presented as an automatic answer.

For municipalities and campuses, the practical question is rarely whether a model can produce a number. It is whether the number can be explained, compared, and acted on by facilities, planning, and sustainability teams. That is where urban building energy modeling, digital twins, and human review still matter.
