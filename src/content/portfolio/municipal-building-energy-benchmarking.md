summary: A capability for turning municipal building portfolios into a comparable, decision-oriented energy picture.
context: Cities manage diverse building stocks and need consistent ways to prioritize upgrades.
challenge: Utility bills, floor area, occupancy, and operational differences make raw consumption difficult to compare.
approach: Structure benchmarking around consistent metrics, data quality checks, and screening for deeper analysis.
value: The intended outcome is a defensible shortlist for audits or retrofits. Portfolio rankings should not be treated as final engineering conclusions.
methods:

  - Data quality review
  - Screening for deeper modeling
