title: Retrofit Pathway Analysis
summary: A structured way to compare envelope, systems, and operational measures over time rather than as a single one-off project.
context: Building owners need retrofit sequences that respect capital cycles, occupancy, and carbon targets.
challenge: Measure-by-measure lists can overstate simple payback and understate interactions between envelope, HVAC, and electrification.
approach: Organize measures into pathways, then evaluate energy, carbon, and implementation implications using transparent assumptions.
value: Expected value is a decision-ready comparison of options. Site-specific performance claims require measured or modeled project data.
methods:
  - Building energy analysis
  - Measure packaging and sequencing
  - CONTENT TO CONFIRM for cost and carbon factors
