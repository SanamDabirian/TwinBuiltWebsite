---
title: CityScale Decarbonization
summary: A district-to-city framing for exploring how building stock change contributes to community carbon goals.
context: Municipal climate plans need building-sector pathways that can be discussed with planning, facilities, and energy partners.
challenge: City-scale targets are easy to state and hard to connect to the timing of envelope upgrades, electrification, and grid change.
approach: Use urban building energy modeling and scenario narratives to make trade-offs visible without implying a single official forecast.
value: Expected value is a shared language for policy and investment conversations. City-wide percentage reductions are not claimed here.
methods:
  - Urban building energy modeling
  - Scenario narratives
  - CONTENT TO CONFIRM for local stock data
